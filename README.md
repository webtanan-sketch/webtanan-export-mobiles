# WooCommerce Customers Export

A simple and practical WordPress plugin for exporting WooCommerce customers’ names and phone numbers.

This plugin helps store managers, marketers, and business owners quickly extract customer contact information from WooCommerce orders and customer records. With just a few clicks, you can generate a clean export file containing customer names and phone numbers for reporting, customer follow-up, SMS marketing, support, or offline usage.

---

## Main Features

- Export WooCommerce customer names
- Export customer phone numbers
- Extract data from WooCommerce orders
- Generate clean and organized customer lists
- Useful for SMS campaigns and customer relationship management
- Simple, lightweight, and easy to use
- Designed for WooCommerce-based online stores
- No complex configuration required

---

## Exported Data

The plugin can export useful customer information such as:

- Customer first name
- Customer last name
- Full customer name
- Billing phone number
- Order-related customer information

---

## Use Cases

This plugin is useful for:

- Preparing customer contact lists
- Sending SMS notifications or campaigns
- Customer support follow-up
- Sales and marketing reports
- Offline customer management
- CRM data preparation
- Store owner reporting

---

## Why Use This Plugin?

WooCommerce stores often contain valuable customer data, but extracting only customer names and phone numbers can be time-consuming.  
This plugin makes the process fast, clean, and simple.

Instead of manually checking orders one by one, store admins can quickly export a structured list of customers and their contact numbers.
